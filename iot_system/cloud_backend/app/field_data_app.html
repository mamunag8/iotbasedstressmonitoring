  • মান টাইপ করে সবুজ 💾 Save & Next ➔ বাটনে চাপুন: প্রথম চাপে একই টবের P1 ➔ P2 হবে; পরের চাপে ম্যাপ অনুযায়ী অটোমেটিক পরবর্তী টবে চলে যাবে!
  • আজ যেসব টবের ১টি গাছ হয়েছে সেগুলোতে ½ এবং ২টি গাছই হলে ✓ চিহ্ন দেখাবে।

Step 0: One-Time 1-Minute Setup
  • Open https://iotserver.mmreza.com/data-app on mobile or PC (use Chrome Add to Home screen for app mode).
  • Tap 🔒 Unlock PIN (top-right) and enter atauniiot to enable saving and editing.
  • Open ⚙️ AI Key and save your OpenRouter (sk-or-v1-...) or Gemini API key with Dual-AI Cross-Check enabled.
Step 1: Walk the 64-Plot Field Layout Systematically (🌱 Growth / 📟 Meters / ⚖️ Lab)
  • The 64-Plot Field Map matches your physical Sera 5 greenhouse layout (IoT Cols 1–8 bottom, Central Pathway middle, Manual Cols 9–16 top).
  • Keep Walk Route on 🐍 Snake Row (1→8, 8→1) for zero backtracking, or tap any plot cell to jump there directly.
  • Pick one parameter or switch to ⚡ Multi-Parameter Sheet, enter the value, and tap 💾 Save & Next ➔: it moves P1 ➔ P2 inside the pot, then advances straight to the next bench plot automatically!
  • Badges track your progress live today: ½ = 1 plant done, ✓ = whole plot complete.
Step 2: Capture Meter LCDs with Dual-AI Photo Verification
  • Tap 📷 Live Camera (AI Read) to photograph Porometer, IR Thermometer, Soil Sensor, Balance, or Leaf Area screens.
  • Manual typed values and AI-extracted values are stored in separate database columns; any Model 1 vs Model 2 disagreement triggers an alert before saving.
  • If greenhouse cellular drops out, entries queue safely in ⚡ Sync and upload automatically when back online.
Step 3: Group/Individual Treatments & HOBO Climate Uploads (🧪 Treat/Env)
  • Use 1-tap presets (All 32 H2S, All 32 Humic, All 64, or Single Planter) to log exact dosing date/time and solution EC/pH.
  • Upload HOBO Temp/Lux and Temp/RH .csv / .xlsx files periodically — duplicates are skipped and VPD (kPa) is auto-calculated.
Step 4: Inline Edit, 5-Sheet Excel & EN/TR A4 Professor Report (📊 View/A4)
  • Filter by experiment, arm, treatment, parameter, or date; click ✏️ Edit on any row to correct values with full edit audit trail.
  • Choose 🇬🇧 English (EN) or 🇹🇷 Türkçe (TR) and tap 🖨️ Print A4 Report for Prof. Dr. Ertan Yıldırım / committee review, or export 📥 Excel (.xlsx) with Mean ± SE and ANOVA-ready sheets.
