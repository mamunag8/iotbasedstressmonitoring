📖 Detailed Materials & Methods and Biophysical Modeling Methodology

Experimental protocol, 48-node RS485 Modbus RTU telemetry architecture, thermodynamic equations, and statistical framework (Atatürk University Faculty of Agriculture — Tomato Salinity x NaHS x Humic Substance Study).

Experimental Protocol & Mathematical Framework

2.1 Experimental Site, Plant Material & 8-Line Factorial Design Yıldırım et al. (2023) Protocol

Experimental Facility & Crop: Conducted at the controlled greenhouse research facility of the Faculty of Agriculture, Atatürk University, Erzurum, Türkiye (39.90 deg N, 41.27 deg E; ~1,850 m altitude). Uniform tomato (Solanum lycopersicum L.) seedlings were transplanted (Reference Transplant Date T0: 16 October 2026) into 7 L pots filled with a standardized 2:1 (v/v) peat:perlite substrate (dry bulk dielectric offset epsilon_b0 = 4.1, bulk density 1.15 g/cm3).

8-Line Fertigation & Biostimulant Regime (24 Plots, n = 3 Replicates/Line):

  • Line 1 (I-C-0, Plots R1–R3): Non-saline Control (0 mM NaCl, target solution EC 2,000 uS/cm, 0 uM NaHS, 0 mL/L Humic).
  • Line 2 (I-C-H2S, Plots R4–R6): Non-saline + Exogenous H2S Donor (0 mM NaCl, 25 uM NaHS, 0 mL/L Humic).
  • Line 3 (I-C-HS, Plots R7–R9): Non-saline + Humic Substance (0 mM NaCl, 0 uM NaHS, 2.5 mL/L LIQHUMUS Liquid 18 NK).
  • Line 4 (I-C-H2S+HS, Plots R10–R12): Non-saline + Combined Biostimulants (0 mM NaCl, 25 uM NaHS + 2.5 mL/L LIQHUMUS NK).
  • Line 5 (I-S-0, Plots R13–R15): Salinized Control (100 mM NaCl [5.844 g/L], target solution EC 11,500 uS/cm, 0 uM NaHS, 0 mL/L Humic).
  • Line 6 (I-S-H2S, Plots R16–R18): Salinized + Exogenous H2S Rescue (100 mM NaCl + 25 uM NaHS).
  • Line 7 (I-S-HS, Plots R19–R21): Salinized + Humic Substance Rescue (100 mM NaCl + 2.5 mL/L LIQHUMUS NK).
  • Line 8 (I-S-H2S+HS, Plots R22–R24): Salinized + Synergistic Co-Application (100 mM NaCl + 25 uM NaHS + 2.5 mL/L LIQHUMUS NK).
Isovalent Mineral Nutrient Matching: Non-humic lines (Lines 1, 2, 5, 6) receive stoichiometric N and K compensation via reagent-grade Urea [CO(NH2)2] + K2SO4 matching the intrinsic N and K content of 2.5 mL/L LIQHUMUS Liquid 18 (NK), isolating supramolecular humic effects from macronutrient nutrition.

2.2 High-Frequency IIoT Sensor Array & Non-Invasive Monitoring Architecture 48 RS485 Modbus RTU Nodes

Root-Zone Dielectric & Ionic Telemetry (Slave IDs 1–24, Plots R1–R24): Each experimental replicate pot is instrumented with an industrial 4-parameter RS485 Modbus RTU frequency-domain/time-domain dielectric probe measuring volumetric water content (VWC, %, resolution 0.1%), root-zone temperature (T_soil, deg C), bulk apparent electrical conductivity (EC_bulk, uS/cm), and apparent salt concentration (TDS_soil, mg/L).

Canopy Phyllosphere Microclimate Array (Slave IDs 101–124, Plots R1_L–R24_L): Paired abaxial/boundary-layer leaf sensors continuously monitor canopy temperature (T_leaf, deg C) and leaf boundary-layer relative humidity (RH_leaf, %).

Edge-to-Cloud Synchronization & Strict Analytical Isolation: A local edge agent buffers PLC and Modbus RTU frames into SQLite and synchronizes batch telemetry to the PostgreSQL 16 cloud database. All stress algorithms, treatment logs, and research diary entries operate in a strictly passive, monitoring-only analytical plane with zero write feedback to PLC solenoid valves (vana1–vana8).

Apparent Root-Zone Ionic Ratio (Salt Coefficient): Salt_Coeff = TDS_soil (mg/L) / max(1, EC_bulk (uS/cm)) [Baseline ~0.55; NaCl accumulation > 0.72]

2.3 Root-Zone Pore-Water Conductivity (ECp), Osmotic Potential & Yield Model Hilhorst (2000) • Maas & Hoffman (1977)

Because bulk soil conductivity (EC_bulk) declines non-linearly during substrate drying even when dissolved NaCl concentration rises, true root-zone salinity exposure is quantified via the linear dielectric-conductivity model of Hilhorst (2000, Soil Sci. Soc. Am. J. 64:1922–1925) coupled with the Topp et al. (1980) polynomial dielectric relationship:

1. Volumetric moisture fraction (clamped at theta >= 0.10 to prevent dry-substrate singularity): theta = max(0.10, VWC_pct / 100.0) 2. Bulk apparent dielectric permittivity (Topp et al., 1980): epsilon_b = 3.03 + 9.3*theta + 146.0*(theta^2) - 76.7*(theta^3) 3. Pore-water dielectric permittivity & temperature compensation to 25 deg C: epsilon_p = 80.3 - 0.37*(T_soil - 20.0) EC_bulk_25 (dS/m) = (EC_bulk_uScm / 1000.0) * [1.0 - 0.020*(T_soil - 25.0)] 4. Hilhorst (2000) Pore-Water EC (ECp) & US Salinity Lab Osmotic Potential (Psi_pi): EC_p (dS/m) = (epsilon_p * EC_bulk_25) / max(0.5, epsilon_b - epsilon_b0) [epsilon_b0 = 4.1] Psi_pi (MPa) = -0.036 * EC_p

Maas & Hoffman (1977) Tomato Relative Yield Potential (Y_rel): Parameterized with the established tomato salinity threshold EC_t = 2.5 dS/m and yield-decline slope s = 9.9% per dS/m:

Y_rel (%) = clamp(100.0 - 9.9 * max(0.0, EC_p - 2.5), 0.0, 100.0)

2.4 Canopy Psychrometrics (VPD), Dew-Point Margin & Empirical CWSI FAO-56 Tetens • Jackson et al. (1981)

Canopy stomatal regulation and atmospheric evaporative demand are derived from paired leaf sensor telemetry (T_leaf, RH_leaf) using the Magnus-Tetens saturation vapor pressure formulation (Allen et al., 1998, FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56) and the empirical Crop Water Stress Index (Jackson et al., 1981; Idso et al., 1981):

1. Saturation & Actual Vapor Pressure at Leaf Boundary Layer: e_s(T_leaf) = 0.61078 * exp[(17.27 * T_leaf) / (T_leaf + 237.3)] (kPa) e_a = e_s(T_leaf) * (RH_leaf / 100.0) (kPa) VPD_leaf = max(0.0, e_s(T_leaf) - e_a) (kPa) 2. Dew-Point Temperature (T_dew) & Fungal Condensation Safety Margin: gamma = ln(max(1.0, RH_leaf) / 100.0) + (17.27 * T_leaf) / (T_leaf + 237.3) T_dew = (237.3 * gamma) / (17.27 - gamma) (deg C) Dew_Margin = T_leaf - T_dew [Risk alert when Dew_Margin < 1.5 deg C] 3. Empirical Canopy-Root CWSI (Jackson et al., 1981): Delta_T = T_leaf - T_soil Delta_T_LL = 1.20 - 1.85 * VPD_leaf (Non-Water-Stressed Lower Baseline) Delta_T_UL = +3.50 deg C (Non-Transpiring Upper Limit) CWSI = clamp((Delta_T - Delta_T_LL) / (Delta_T_UL - Delta_T_LL), 0.0, 1.0)

2.5 Thermodynamic H2S / HS- Equilibrium Speciation in Substrate Solution Henderson-Hasselbalch pKa1(T) Model

When sodium hydrosulfide (NaHS) is delivered into the root zone, it dissociates rapidly into dissolved molecular hydrogen sulfide gas (H2S(aq), the uncharged lipophilic signaling species that permeates root aquaporins and plasma membranes) and hydrosulfide anion (HS-). The temperature-dependent first dissociation constant pKa1(T) and bioavailable [H2S]_active concentration are computed continuously per irrigation line:

1. Temperature-Corrected First Acid Dissociation Constant (25 deg C reference pKa1 = 7.05): pKa1(T_soil) = 7.05 - 0.014 * (T_soil - 25.0) 2. Molecular H2S(aq) Molar Fraction at Reference / Measured Root-Zone pH: f_H2S = 1.0 / [1.0 + 10^(pH_soil - pKa1(T_soil))] f_HS_minus = 1.0 - f_H2S 3. Bioavailable Dissolved Signaling Gas Concentration: [H2S]_active (uM) = [NaHS]_applied (uM) * f_H2S (At pH 6.0 and T_soil = 22 deg C: pKa1 = 7.09, f_H2S = 92.5%, so 25 uM NaHS yields 23.1 uM active H2S(aq))

2.6 Composite Plant Stress Index (PSI), Vitality (PVI), Recovery (SRI) & Statistical Pipeline Multi-Subscore Integration & ANOVA / R2

Four normalized physiological sub-scores (each scaled 0–100) integrate root-zone osmotic/halophytic load (S_salinity, weight 0.30), soil matric water deficit/waterlogging (S_water, weight 0.25), phyllosphere evaporative demand (S_vpd, weight 0.25), and canopy stomatal/thermal stress (S_thermal, weight 0.20):

1. Composite Plant Stress Index (PSI) & Plant Vitality Index (PVI): PSI (0-100) = 0.30*S_salinity + 0.25*S_water + 0.25*S_vpd + 0.20*S_thermal PVI (0-100) = 100.0 - PSI Stress Classification: 0-20 Optimal | 20-45 Mild | 45-70 Moderate | >70 Severe 2. Biostimulant Stress Recovery Index (SRI %) for Salinized Treatments T in {Line 6, Line 7, Line 8}: SRI_T (%) = clamp(((PSI_Line5_SaltControl - PSI_T) / max(5.0, PSI_Line5_SaltControl - PSI_Line1_NonSaline)) * 100.0, -100.0, 100.0) 3. Cumulative Stress Degree-Hours (SDH) & Root-to-Canopy Coupling: SDH (deg C * h) = Sum [ max(0.0, (T_leaf - T_soil) - 1.0) * Delta_t_hours ] OLS Linear Regression: CWSI = beta_0 + beta_1 * EC_p (with coefficient of determination R2 across n = 24 plots)
